# Data protection

> A call about whose personal data the company handles, what it does with it, and who else receives it.

## What it means

A decision whose subject is personal data: what a visitor or a customer gives up, what the company does with it and for how long, which parties receive it and on what terms, and what the privacy page says about it. It is this kind even when it shapes what gets built, because what was decided is what happens to someone's data.

The license and what adoption costs are Terms; what core holds is Vocabulary, even where a type is about personal data; how the tooling is built is Architecture.
