# Team

> A group that gathers people and agents for the work on CompanyGraph, and stands outside the disciplinary line.

## What it means

A team is for the work on CompanyGraph. It stands outside the disciplinary line because nothing CompanyGraph publishes says it hires anyone, appraises anyone or sets anyone's objectives. A unit that did those things would stand in the line, and would not be a team.
