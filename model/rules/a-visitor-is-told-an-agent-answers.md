# A visitor is told an agent answers

> A visitor who asks a chat of ours is told, before the first message is sent and in the language of the page, that an agent writes the answer from the model and which company's language model it goes through.

## Why

Article 50 of the European Union's AI Act obliges whoever provides an AI system that interacts with people to inform them that it does, no later than the first interaction, unless that is obvious from the circumstances; it applies since August 2, 2026, and reaches a company outside the Union whose system's output is used in it. Our chat is placed on our own pages under our own name on top of Anthropic's Claude, which makes us the provider of that system, and a fluent answer in the company's own voice is not obviously an AI's. The Act also asks that the text such a system generates be marked as generated in a form a machine can read; whether an answer streamed to a page and kept nowhere is such text is not settled, and this rule does not reach it until it is.
