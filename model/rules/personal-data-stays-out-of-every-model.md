# Personal data stays out of every model

> No model holds personal data about anyone but its owner describing themselves and the people who consented to be described in it.

## Why

A model is published, read by agents and visitors, and kept in a history that never forgets a state, so personal data written into it could never be taken back. The records a company keeps about the people it serves, its customers, guests, patients and visitors, belong in the systems the model describes and never in the model itself; the model says that such a system holds them and what it does with them. The owner describing themselves, and a person who consented to a profile, chose to be in it.
