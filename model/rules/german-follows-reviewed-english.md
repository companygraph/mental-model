# German follows reviewed English

> German is made only from English the Owner has reviewed, never from a draft.

## Why

A translation of a draft is translated twice: once now and again when the English changes under review, and the second time a sentence that moved in English is easily left behind in German. Translating once, from the reviewed text, makes the German follow a fixed text.
