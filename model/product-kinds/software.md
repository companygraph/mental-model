# Software

> What CompanyGraph ships to be installed and run: the metamodel package with its checks, server, plugin and tooling.

## What it means

A product of this kind is opened by the people who run a company and the agents beside them, by vendoring it into their own repository and running what it ships. CompanyGraph Core is this kind. There is no other kind yet; consulting, were it ever a product, would be a second.
