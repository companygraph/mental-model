# Planner

> The seat that cuts an approved specification into task briefs, each whole on its own, and writes none of them.

## What it takes

A specification the Owner has approved, and the repository the work lands in, including the rules that bind it.

## What it produces

An ordered set of task briefs. Each is the whole of its own requirements, names the interfaces the briefs around it produce and consume, and states how its holder can tell it is done. An entry of a model gets a brief of its own, because the Owner decides one entry at a time. The order is the order they can be worked in, not the order they were thought of.

## What it never does

- Never re-opens a decision the specification took.
- Never writes a brief that depends on a conversation its holder did not have.
- Never writes the change.
- Never plans a task whose completion cannot be checked.
- Never puts two entries of a model in one brief.
