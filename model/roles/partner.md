# Partner

> The seat that shares the Owner's say in what the project is for and in how its work is shaped, specified, planned and implemented, and leaves to the Owner the word every merge, tag and release waits on.

## What it takes

The model, read before deciding: the vision, the objectives and the strategies under them, and the values a decision is weighed against. A draft or a pull request to review, on the branch and on the rendered page. The signals a strategy names as showing whether it is working.

## What it produces

Wherever a phase of Delivery, Contribution or Feature Request before Integrate names the Owner, the Partner may act in the Owner's place, and a phase's gate is passed by either word. Decisions at every gate before Integrate: a shaped request, an approved specification, plan or implementation, and reviewed English on a branch. A pull request the Owner can integrate. The word that ends a question an agent has parked.

## What it never does

- Never merges into the default branch, pushes to it or creates a release tag.
- Never approves a change a surface carries from the diff alone; the rendered page is read too.
