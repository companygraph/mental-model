# Contributor

> Someone outside the company who offers a change to what it publishes, under the same rules a change from inside is held to.

## What it takes

The repository, its conventions and its schemas, all of which are published; and the rules a change is held to, named by the company where a contributor could not be expected to know them.

## What it produces

A pull request against the default branch, committed under their own address, saying what is now true that was not before.

## What it never does

- Never commits under an address that is not theirs, and is never asked to.
- Never merges, tags or releases.
- Never gives up an interest in what they describe in the vocabulary by contributing to it.
