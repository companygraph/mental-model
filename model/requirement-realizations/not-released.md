# Not released

> No release meets any part of the requirement.

## What it means

A requirement is Not released while no release of the product it applies to meets any part of it, and its page lists no `## Evidence`; work for it that no release carries yet, and what stands in for it today, is said in its `## Realization`. A repository that deploys rather than tags, as the site and its deployments do, is released at the commit it has deployed. A page is at the level from when the requirement is written, and leaves it for Partly released or Released when a release ships what meets it. While a requirement is Not released, a reader may not rely on any release meeting it.
