# Not released

> No release meets any part of the requirement.

## What it means

A requirement is Not released while no release of the product it applies to meets any part of it, and its page lists no `## Evidence`; work for it on `main` that no tag names is said in its `## Realization`, if anywhere. A page is at the level from when the requirement is written, and leaves it for Partly released or Released when a release ships what meets it. While a requirement is Not released, a reader may not rely on any release meeting it.
