# Answer

> Give the asker something they can act on, whether or not anything will be built.

## What it takes

A classified gap with its reason.

## Activities

1. The Owner writes the answer in the issue: what was decided, why, and what the asker can do now.
2. Where the gap is real and will be filled, the Owner says which release it is aimed at only if one has been chosen, and otherwise says that it has not been.
3. Where the gap is real and will not be filled, the Owner says what would change that.
4. Where there is no gap, the Owner names the type that already carries the fact and how it is written.
5. The Owner closes the issue.

## What it produces

| Deliverable | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Answer | The decision, its reason and what the asker can do now, written where the request was made |

## What it never does

- Never closes with a label and no sentence.
- Never leaves the asker without something to do, even where the answer is no.
- Never promises a date that has not been chosen.

## Gate

To leave Answer, all of these hold:

- The answer says what was decided and why, in the issue.
- The asker has something to do next, or is told plainly that there is nothing.
- No date appears that has not actually been chosen.

Where they cannot be met, the issue stays open; an answer nobody can act on is not an answer and closing it does not make it one.
