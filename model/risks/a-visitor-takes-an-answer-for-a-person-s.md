# A visitor takes an answer for a person's

> A visitor who asks the chat on one of our pages reads the answer as something a person at the company wrote to them.

## Cause

The chat answers on the company's own page, in the company's own first person, fluently and in the language the visitor wrote in, and nothing in the answer itself says that an agent wrote it. What says so is a notice under the input, which a visitor may not read, or may read without learning from it that an AI is answering, because it names a product rather than saying the word.

## Consequence

The visitor holds the company to a sentence no person said, and the company has not told a person it meets that they were talking to an AI, which is the first thing the AI Act asks of a provider of such a system. The test the vision rests on, the same question put to a person and to an agent and the two answers compared, loses its meaning for a visitor who cannot tell which of the two they got.
