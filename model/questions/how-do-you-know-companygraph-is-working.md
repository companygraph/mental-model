# How do you know CompanyGraph is working?

> Grounded Answer Rate watches whether the chat's answers rest on the model and Carried Claim Rate whether they keep to it, as the objective of an agent answering as well as the person who runs the company asks; Merges Past Their Checks watches whether our merges keep to the gates a person holds; and the DORA KPIs define what is watched in how our changes reach production. The model holds their definitions and none of their values: the chat's report computes Grounded Answer Rate's and counts what Carried Claim Rate reads, the weekly kpi workflow counts Merges Past Their Checks, nothing measures the DORA KPIs yet, and our other objectives have no KPI yet.
