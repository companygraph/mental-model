# How do you know CompanyGraph is working?

> Grounded Answer Rate watches whether the chat's answers rest on the model, as the objective of an agent answering as well as the person who runs the company asks, and the DORA KPIs define what is watched in how our changes reach production. The model holds their definitions and none of their values: the chat's report computes Grounded Answer Rate's, nothing measures the DORA KPIs yet, and our other objectives have no KPI yet.

## Rests on

| Type | Entity | Owner | For |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| strategic-objective | An agent answers as well as the person who runs the company | | the objective the chat is held to |
| kpi | Grounded Answer Rate | | whether answers rest on the model, and where its value is |
| kpi | Deployment Frequency | | how often a change ships |
| kpi | Change Lead Time | | how long a change takes to ship |
| kpi | Change Fail Rate | | how often a deployment fails |
| kpi | Failed Deployment Recovery Time | | how soon a failed deployment is recovered |
| kpi | Deployment Rework Rate | | how much shipping is unplanned rework |
