# A company of more than one person describes its units and who answers to whom

> A company of more than one person writes down its units and the teams drawn from them, the jobs its people are employed in, who answers to whom and who sets the discipline of whose work, and which positions it is looking to fill.

## Description

The organization pack, taken beside core: a group is a unit of the company or a team drawn from its units, of a kind the company names, and a job is what a person is employed as wherever they sit. A person sits in a unit in a job, and the disciplinary line runs from each person to their unit's lead and up through the units; the professional line runs from a group to the jobs it guides, wherever the people in them sit. A group names the positions it is looking to fill, a staff unit stands beside the head it serves, and a rank on each group is the company's own order.

It stops at the structure. It draws no org chart, which is a renderer's to draw from the pages, and holds no headcount and no cost.
