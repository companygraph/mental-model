# Every gate an agent's work passes is held by a person

> Agents do the work of a company's processes, and at every gate that work reaches, a person who answers for it decides whether it goes on; no agent's work passes a gate on the strength of the checks alone, whether a person asked for it or a workflow started it.

## What it makes true

Each gate a process names has a person among its approvers, and every passage through it can be traced to the person who let it through and when. A change to the model is one such gate, a release another, and a workflow that runs on its own meets the same gates as work somebody asked for. It fails the day agent work passes a gate that no person approved, or passes one on an approval nobody can trace.

What falls outside: how the person judges what is in front of them, which is for the strategies and the tooling beneath this objective to serve, and how fast or how often gates are passed, because a gate measured by throughput rewards letting things through. Also outside: answers, which change nothing and pass no gate.
