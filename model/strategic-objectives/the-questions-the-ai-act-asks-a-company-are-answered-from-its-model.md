# The questions the AI Act asks a company are answered from its model

> What the European Union's AI Act asks of a company that provides or deploys an AI system — what the system rests on, who oversees it, what it tells the people it meets, what it keeps and who else sees what passes through it — is answered from the company's model, by a person or an agent, with the page each answer rests on, rather than from a file assembled for the occasion.

## What it makes true

Each obligation the Act puts to a provider or a deployer of an AI system has a type to carry the company's answer, and the answer can be followed to a page: the rule the obligation became in the company's own words, the control that keeps it and whether a machine or a person carries it out, the risk it guards against and the seat that answers for it, the processor each piece of data reaches, the KPI that assesses the control, and the person at every gate an agent's work passes. We are the first instance of it. The chat on our own site is an AI system a visitor meets, so the Act's questions are put to this model first, and a question we cannot answer from it is a finding about the vocabulary before it is anything else.

It holds when a company that keeps its model answers a regulator, a customer or its own board under the Act by pointing at pages, and when an obligation that newly applies to it enters the model as a rule with nothing else built to hold it. It fails the day a question the Act asks has no type to carry the answer, or the day the answer lives in a document beside the model that the model does not name.

What falls outside: whether any company, this one included, complies. The model holds what a company does and how it is kept, and whether that satisfies the Act is a judgment a lawyer or an authority makes, which the model is opened for and never replaces. Also outside: the uses the Act treats as high-risk and the systems it regulates as products, until a company that runs one keeps a model, because a type enters the vocabulary when an instance had to be written with it and none of ours runs one. Other laws fall outside as well. The model answers data-protection law where its processing activities and processors reach, and the Act is named here because it is the law that asks about an AI system as such.
