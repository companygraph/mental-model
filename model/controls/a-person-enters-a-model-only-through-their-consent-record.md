# A person enters a model only through their consent record

> Nothing about a person is written into a model until the Owner has stated that person's consent, and the consent is recorded on the source every fact about them names.

## How it is carried out

The Owner, through the consent procedure that the profile and company procedures run as a step, before anything drawn from a source about a person is written: the procedure proposes that a person drawn from any source needs their own consent, takes only a consent the Owner states, who gave it, in what capacity, when and how, and writes it as one sentence on the source; a consent the Owner cannot state is not given, and nothing that rested on it is written. The Owner describing themselves needs none. It holds the procedures and nothing else: a page written by hand about a person passes through no consent step, and a consent withdrawn later takes the person out of the current state while every earlier state still holds them.
