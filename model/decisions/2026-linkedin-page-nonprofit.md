# The LinkedIn company page is typed Nonprofit

> We give the LinkedIn company page the type Nonprofit, because it is the one type the network offers that claims no business, and nothing here is sold.

## The question

Which organization type the LinkedIn company page carries. The network demands one from a closed list when a page is made, every type on the list names a legal form, and no CompanyGraph company has been founded: whatever the page says is inexact, so the choice is which inexactness to publish. It had to be decided when the page was made, on 2026-09-28.

## Alternatives

| Option | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| Self-Employed | It says CompanyGraph is a business its owner runs for a living, and nobody runs it as one: nothing is sold and nobody is paid. |
| Sole Proprietorship, Partnership or Privately Held | Each names a founded company that does not exist, and a reader would look for a register entry and find none. |
| Leave the type empty | The network does not publish a page without one. |

## Why

Nonprofit is the one type that says the page earns nothing, and that is true: the pages say plainly that nothing is being sold, and whether this becomes consulting at all is still open. Every other type claims either a business or a legal entity, and each of those is false in a way a reader could check.

## Consequences

The page names a legal form no entity has, and we accept that as the least false of the types the network offers. It stays right only while nothing is sold. The call is revisited the day Whether this is a business is answered is settled, or a CompanyGraph company is founded, whichever comes first, since either makes a truer type available.
