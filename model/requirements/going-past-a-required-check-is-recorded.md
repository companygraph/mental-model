# Going past a required check is recorded

> Every change applied while a required check had not passed is recorded with the check it went past and the account that applied it.

## Why

A gate an admin can pass stays honest only where every pass is counted, and Merges Past Their Checks counts them.

## Fit criterion

Every change of a week applied past a required check can be listed with that check and that account. Today GitHub's rule suites meet it.

## Applies to

| Type | Entity |
| --- | --- |
| feature | Checks an instance runs |
