# Only named accounts apply a change

> A change is applied only by an account the company names for it.

## Why

Applying a change is what makes it the model's, and who may do that is the company's decision.

## Fit criterion

An account that may propose a change is refused when it applies one. Today the push restriction to the Owner's account meets it in an organization's repositories.
